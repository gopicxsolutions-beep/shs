1. Who we are

NavaSakhi (also referred to as SHG Saathi) is a digital platform for Self-Help Groups (SHGs) and women-led microenterprises, covering savings, loans, meetings, livelihoods, a marketplace, government schemes, training, and federation monitoring (SHG → CRP → CLF → Admin). This policy explains what information the NavaSakhi mobile app and website (navasakhi.com) collect, how that information is used, and who it is shared with.

2. Information we collect

We collect the following categories of information when you register and use the app:

2.1 Account and profile information

  • Full name
  • Mobile phone number (used to sign in via a one-time password — we do not use or store account passwords)
  • Village, mandal/block, and district
  • Your Self-Help Group (SHG) membership and role (member, leader, CRP, CLF, or admin)

2.2 Baseline survey (members and leaders)

When you join, you complete a one-time baseline survey used for program monitoring and research. This is the most sensitive information the app collects, and includes:

  • Age, education level, marital status, household size, and caste/community
  • Annual household income and primary income source
  • Microenterprise details: sector, years operating, monthly revenue, employees, registration status
  • Digital access and literacy: smartphone/internet access, apps used, digital-payment habits
  • Financial inclusion: bank account status, credit access, savings patterns, awareness of government schemes
  • Entrepreneurial skills, training needs, and self-assessed empowerment/decision-making role in the household
  • Your typed signature and consent confirmation for this survey

This survey is collected only with your explicit consent at the time you complete it, and — unlike most other data in the app — is not shared with other members of your SHG or even your own SHG leader. See Section 6.

2.3 Financial and transactional information

  • Savings entries: amount, payment mode, frequency, and verification status
  • Loan applications and repayments: purpose, amount, EMI, tenure, outstanding balance, and status
  • Your SHG's group ledger and cashbook entries
  • Your SHG's own bank account number and IFSC code (entered by the SHG's leader for the group's shared treasury account — this is the group's bank detail, not an individual member's personal bank account)
  • Livelihood/microenterprise investment and revenue figures you choose to record

NavaSakhi's in-app "payments" feature (e.g. UPI/QR) currently records a payment reference for your own tracking; it does not connect to a bank or process real money movement.

2.4 Marketplace

If you list or buy products in the in-app marketplace, we collect product photos and descriptions, your name as shown to other users, order details (buyer/seller, quantity, price), reviews you write, and — if you provide one — a UPI payment ID or note you choose to display so buyers can pay you directly. Marketplace payments are arranged directly between buyer and seller; NavaSakhi does not process or hold this money.

2.5 Meetings, schemes, and training

  • Meeting attendance records
  • Government scheme applications and their supporting details
  • Training course progress and quiz results

2.6 Documents and files you upload

Documents you upload for your SHG (e.g. meeting minutes, ID proofs) and photos you upload for marketplace listings are stored in our backend (Supabase Storage) and are only accessible to the people described in Section 6.

2.7 AI Advisor conversations

If you use the Financial, Scheme, or Market AI Advisor chat features, we log your questions and the AI's responses (including a short conversation history for context) to provide the feature, moderate misuse, and improve reliability. See Section 7 for how this is processed.

2.8 Support requests

If you contact support in-app, we collect the subject, description, category, and any follow-up messages you send in that ticket.

2.9 Voice input

If you use the Voice Assistant or Voice Support feature, your speech is converted to text entirely on your device using your phone's built-in speech-recognition engine. The audio itself is never recorded, saved, or sent to NavaSakhi or any other party — only the resulting text is used, the same as if you had typed it.

2.10 Automatically collected information

If crash/error reporting is enabled by the app operator, basic technical diagnostic information (such as the type of crash and which screen it occurred on) may be automatically collected to help us fix bugs. See Section 7.

3. Information we do not collect

  • We do not track your GPS location or any other location data.
  • We do not use advertising or ad-tracking software, and we do not show third-party ads.
  • We do not use general-purpose analytics or tracking SDKs (e.g. Google Analytics, Facebook/Meta tools).
  • We do not record or store your voice — only on-device speech-to-text output, as described above.
  • We do not ask for or store your account password — sign-in uses a one-time SMS code only.

5. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account and SHG membership
  • To operate the app's core features: savings, loans, meetings, marketplace, schemes, training, and reporting
  • To let your SHG track its collective savings, loans, and meeting records, consistent with how SHGs already operate transparently in person
  • To allow federation staff (CRP/CLF/Admin) to monitor and support SHGs across the program
  • To respond to support requests
  • To send SMS one-time passwords for sign-in
  • To improve and troubleshoot the app (e.g. via optional crash reporting, if enabled)
  • To meet program monitoring, reporting, and research obligations (baseline survey data)

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

6. Who can see your information

NavaSakhi is built around the same transparency SHGs already practise in person, plus program oversight by federation staff:

  • Members of your own SHG can see each other's savings entries, loan records, meeting attendance, and the group ledger — the same way these are read aloud at in-person meetings. They cannot see each other's baseline survey answers.
  • Your SHG's bank account/IFSC is visible only to your SHG's leader and to federation staff — not to ordinary members.
  • Your baseline survey (Section 2.2) is visible only to you and to federation staff (CRP/CLF/Admin) — not to your fellow members or even your own SHG leader.
  • Federation staff (CRP, CLF, and Admin roles) can see savings, loans, meetings, ledgers, and reports across all SHGs in the program, for monitoring and support purposes.
  • Marketplace listings, orders, and reviews are visible to any NavaSakhi user browsing the marketplace, across all SHGs — not limited to your own group.
  • Support tickets and AI Advisor logs are visible to you and to staff, for support and misuse-monitoring purposes.

8. Data retention and deletion

We retain your SHG's financial and program records (savings, loans, meetings, ledger, marketplace orders, baseline survey) for as long as your SHG participates in the program, consistent with the recordkeeping SHGs already maintain. AI Advisor conversation logs are automatically deleted after 180 days.

Account deactivation: at this time, NavaSakhi supports deactivating an account (which disables sign-in) but does not offer a self-service permanent deletion of historical group records, because those records form part of your SHG's shared financial history. If you would like your account deactivated or have questions about your data, contact us using the details in Section 13.

9. Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) between the app and our servers, and database-level access controls (Row-Level Security) that restrict who can read or write each piece of data, independent of the app's own screens. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or request deactivation of your data. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below. We will respond consistent with applicable law, including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

11. Children's privacy

NavaSakhi is intended for adult members of Self-Help Groups and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 18.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the effective date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the app.
