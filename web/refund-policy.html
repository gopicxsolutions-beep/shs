1. How the NavaSakhi marketplace works

The NavaSakhi marketplace lets any member list a product and any other member, across every Self-Help Group on the platform, browse and buy it. It is a peer-to-peer marketplace, not a storefront run by NavaSakhi: NavaSakhi does not manufacture, stock, inspect, or ship any product listed.

NavaSakhi does not process or hold payment for any order. There is no payment gateway integrated into the app. A seller may display a UPI ID and an optional note on her product listing, and a buyer pays that seller directly — by scanning a UPI QR code, opening her own UPI app, or another method (such as cash) the two of them agree on. Money for a marketplace purchase never passes through NavaSakhi at any point.

2. Cancelling an order

An order moves through four stages: New → Packed → Shipped → Delivered. You (the buyer) can cancel your own order, free of charge, from the "My Purchases" tab of the Marketplace Orders screen, at any time while it is still in the "New" stage — i.e. before the seller has packed it. Cancelling restores the item to the seller's stock automatically.

Once the seller marks the order "Packed," in-app cancellation is no longer available. If you need to cancel after that point, contact the seller directly — see Section 5.

3. Returns and refunds

Because NavaSakhi never holds a buyer's payment, it has no money in its possession to refund, and cannot process a refund on a seller's behalf. Any return or refund for a delivered order must be arranged directly between the buyer and the seller, by mutual agreement — the same as any other direct, in-person or UPI-based purchase between two people.

Before contacting the seller about a return or refund, it helps to have ready:

  • The order details, visible under "My Purchases"
  • The seller's name and (if you used it) the UPI ID you paid
  • A clear description of the issue, and a photo if the item is damaged or different from what was listed

NavaSakhi does not guarantee that a seller will accept a return or issue a refund, and is not a party to the payment itself. We encourage buyers to review a product's photos, description, price, and the seller's existing ratings/reviews carefully before placing an order, and to confirm the exact quantity and total amount before paying.

4. Damaged, defective, or wrong item

If an item arrives damaged, defective, or different from what was ordered, contact the seller as soon as possible with a description and photo of the issue and request a replacement, return, or refund directly with them. Most sellers on NavaSakhi are individual Self-Help Group members selling their own goods, not registered retailers — response times and return terms can vary seller to seller, and NavaSakhi does not set or enforce a uniform return window.

5. Disputes between buyer and seller

If a buyer and seller cannot resolve a return or refund between themselves, either party can raise it through the in-app Support feature. NavaSakhi staff can review the order record (status, quantity, amount, and timestamps) to help clarify what happened, but — because no payment ever passes through NavaSakhi — staff cannot reverse a UPI/cash payment that was made directly between buyer and seller, and does not act as a payment arbitrator or escrow agent.

6. Ratings and reviews

A buyer who has ordered a product may leave a star rating and written review on that product's page after purchase. Reviews are public and visible to every NavaSakhi user browsing that listing — a genuine, good-faith review of your experience (including about how a return or refund request was handled) is a legitimate and encouraged way to inform other buyers, and helps sellers build trust on the platform.

7. What this policy does not cover

  • SHG savings, loan repayments, and group ledger entries — these are not marketplace purchases and are governed by your SHG's own recordkeeping, not this policy.
  • Government scheme applications and payments made outside the app.
  • Any payment made through a third-party app or service NavaSakhi does not operate.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example if NavaSakhi ever integrates a real payment gateway or an in-app return workflow. If we make material changes, we will update the effective date above.
